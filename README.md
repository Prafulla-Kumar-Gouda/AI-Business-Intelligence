# AI Business Intelligence — Universal Data Analytics Platform

An interactive Business Intelligence platform that transforms raw business
datasets into meaningful insights through automated data cleaning, analytics,
customer segmentation, sales forecasting, recommendations, and AI-powered
business insights.

## 🚀 Live Demo

[Open the Live Dashboard](https://ai-business-intelligence-prafulla.streamlit.app/)

## 📌 Overview

AI Business Intelligence is a data analytics and decision-support platform
built with Python and Streamlit.

The platform allows users to upload business datasets in CSV or Excel format
and automatically process the data to generate useful business insights.

Instead of manually performing multiple analytical tasks, the platform
combines data preparation, business analytics, machine learning, forecasting,
recommendations, and interactive visualization into a single dashboard.

## 🧠 Intelligence Modules

### 1. Customer Segmentation

The platform analyzes customer purchasing behavior and creates meaningful
customer segments using RFM-based features and machine learning clustering.

The resulting segments include:

- VIP / Loyal Customers
- Potential High Value
- Regular Customers
- At Risk / Inactive

This helps businesses understand customer behavior and identify valuable
customer groups.

### 2. Sales Forecasting

Historical revenue data is aggregated into monthly sales trends and used to
generate future revenue forecasts.

The forecasting module provides:

- Historical monthly revenue
- Forecasted revenue
- Forecast summary
- Forecast accuracy metrics
- Business interpretation of forecast results

### 3. Recommendation Engine

The recommendation engine converts purchasing patterns and customer
segments into actionable recommendations.

It provides:

- Customer recommendations
- Product recommendations
- Product-pair opportunities
- Business recommendations
- Product strategy insights

### 4. AI Business Insights

The platform converts analytical results into easy-to-understand business
insights and recommendations.

These insights help users understand:

- What is happening in the business
- Which customers require attention
- Which products are performing well
- Where potential opportunities exist
- What actions a business could consider
